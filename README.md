## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Cognition Method**. This trainer enhances exploration and puzzle-solving in Cognition Method by adjusting movement, protecting the player from hazardous falls, and providing quality-of-life controls for navigating the space station and the Artifact. It is designed for a single-player experience with no conventional combat system.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed way to explore Cognition Method, study its surreal environments, revisit story details, or experiment with gravity-based puzzles without repeatedly losing progress.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement option to adjust traversal speed while exploring the space station and the Artifact. Enhanced movement is useful for covering long corridors and revisiting areas, while position locking can help when examining a puzzle from a particular angle.

### Tools & Inventory

Cognition Method does not use a traditional item inventory or equipment system. Progress is centered on environmental interaction, puzzle objects, recordings, notes, and other discoveries. The trainer therefore focuses on movement and puzzle assistance rather than item duplication or resource editing.

### World & Environment

The game features two contrasting settings: a bright human space station and a cold, minimalistic Artifact filled with impossible geometry. Use exploration options carefully when searching for narrative clues or hidden rooms, and disable movement changes before activating gravity-based mechanisms.

### Combat & Weapons

Cognition Method has no conventional combat system. The main challenges come from environmental puzzles, altered gravity, navigation, unsettling discoveries, and the risk of falling or becoming disoriented in shifting spaces.
